\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This section is a recap of what this thesis achieved along with the result to characterise the deliverable.

\subsection{Hardware delivered}

Firstly, this thesis was quite involved in term of hardware and altough the mechanical design was aldready done and provided, a lot of work was required to bring it from a fancy paper weight to a robot that could be controlled.
The platform is a biped weighed at 15\,kg. It carries four AKE90-8 QDD actuators (\cref{tab:motors}) and two AK60-39 for the hip roll.
Its 13S2P pack holds 468\,Wh and is rated 100\,A continuous and 360\,A for 2\,s (\cref{sec:hw-battery}).
A Raspberry Pi 3B run the control loop at 200\,Hz on a stock Linux. The platform can be backdriven and playback a recorded gait along with running the inference for a given policy.
The platform was characterised to bound the performance envelope and to inform the training.

\subsection{Running speed ceiling estimation}

The physical speed ceiling was established independently of reinforcement learning and bounded to 6\,m/s (\cref{sec:speed-ceiling}).
The bound was established by first principles and validated in simulation.
The bound is a function of the leg workspace, the impulse it can deliver and the mass distribution of the robot.

\subsection{Reinforcement learning results}
\label{sec:rl-results}
The infrasctucture to train a policy on the measured plant was built and tested.
Multiple policy where trained with varying result that described in this chapter.
The main result is that a the pipeline can converge to a running policy on the measured plant and reach speed of 3 m/s in simulation.
