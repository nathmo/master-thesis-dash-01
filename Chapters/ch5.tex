\subsection{Hardware delivered}

Firstly, this thesis was quite involved in term of hardware and altough the mechanical design was aldready done and provided, a lot of work was required to bring it from a fancy paper weight to a robot that could be controlled.
The platform is a biped weighed at 15\,kg. It carries four AKE90-8 QDD actuators (\cref{tab:motors}) and two AK60-39 for the hips abduction.
Its 13S2P pack holds 468\,Wh and is rated 100\,A continuous and 360\,A for 2\,s (\cref{sec:hw-battery}).
A Raspberry Pi 3B+ run the control loop at 200\,Hz on a stock Linux. The platform can be backdriven and playback a recorded gait.
The platform was caracterized to bound the performance envelope and to inform the training.
The measured plant is reported in \cref{tab:plant-id}.
