\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

DASH-01 is a 15\,kg biped with six quasi-direct-drive actuators, a parallel 4-bar knee and a
passive ankle. It was built at REHAssist to answer one question: how fast can a
cheap custom biped run? This thesis, altough RL oriented in the title, takes the platform from a partially finished mechanical design
to a modelled machine that can be simulated and for which RL policy can be trainied against. 
Finally, this thesis asses the performance envelope of the platform and the policy.

The work covers the whole chain. A CAD-to-MJCF pipeline closes the kinematic loop that URDF cannot
express. A measurement campaign to model the plant parameter. 
A first-principles feasibility sweep bounds the running speed the mechanism admits.

The curriculum has been shown to converge to a running policy within literrature reported training steps.

On this platform the binding constraint is the plant rather than the algorithm. The thesis
therefore ends in a ranked set of mechanical changes rather than in a controller.
